

	
	

Functional Requirements for Sales Intelligence & AI Decisioning
For - NTT DATA North America
Draft 3 - September 16, 2026
Background
Syren is proposing a role-based sales intelligence experience for NTT DATA North America. The solution will use Salesforce pipeline and opportunity history to help sales users see risk early, understand why it is happening, decide what to do next, and find growth opportunities in existing accounts.
This scope focuses only on the functional experience for three personas. The strongest focus is on charts, actions, decisions, and AI-assisted features.
Problem/Need
Sales data is available, but users still need a clear answer to four questions: What needs attention? Why does it need attention? What decision should I make? What action should happen next?
The proposed experience should convert pipeline signals into a role-based workbench so that sales executives, managers, and leadership can move from information to action without reading large reports or manually comparing many records.
Functional Requirements
Core Use Cases
• Anomaly Detection: Find unusual deal, rep, account, pipeline, value, margin, or process behavior. Show the reason, evidence, severity, and a suggested next review step.
• Deal Closure Prediction: Show the likelihood of each deal closing, place it in a simple risk band, and show the strongest positive and negative factors behind the score.
• Cross-Sell / Upsell: Find products or services that an account does not buy today but similar accounts often buy. Show confidence, supporting methods, and a simple reason for the recommendation.
The anomaly engine is a review signal, not a final verdict. Cross-sell recommendations are growth ideas, not guaranteed sales. Risk thresholds and business rules should be configurable with NTT during implementation.

Common Functional Pattern
Each persona should use the same simple flow. The amount of detail changes by role, but the flow stays consistent.
Step
	Functional behavior
	
1. Observe
	KPI cards, charts, alerts, and exception lists show what changed or needs attention.
	
2. Understand
	The user opens a deal, account, rep, or business area to see evidence, history, benchmark, and model drivers.
	
3. Decide
	A decision card explains the issue or opportunity, business impact, confidence/severity, and available choices.
	
4. Act
	The user creates the next step: update a deal, assign a review, request support, launch a recovery action, or pursue a cross-sell idea.
	
5. Track
	The system keeps owner, due date, status, comments, decision reason, and outcome so the action can be followed to closure.
	

Common Decision Card - Required Fields
Field
	Requirement
	
Decision title
	Short statement such as “Review high-value deal at risk” or “Consider cross-sell: Managed Services”.
	
Why now
	The event or condition that caused the card to appear: risk drop, stage stall, anomaly, budget gap, or high-confidence growth recommendation.
	
Business impact
	Deal value, GP, target gap, account value, or other amount affected.
	
Evidence
	Source fields, history, peer benchmark, anomaly evidence, or model drivers used to support the card.
	
Confidence / severity
	High/Medium/Low risk, anomaly severity, or Very High/High/Medium cross-sell confidence.
	
Recommended options
	Two or more clear user choices. Example: review deal, update forecast, request support, monitor, dismiss with reason.
	
Owner and due date
	Person responsible for the next step and when it should be completed.
	
Decision reason
	Required short note when the user accepts, rejects, dismisses, or changes the suggested action.
	
Status and outcome
	New, In Review, Actioned, Monitoring, Closed/Dismissed; final outcome stored for later learning and reporting.
	

Shared Filters and Navigation
• Global filters: quarter, month, country, region, industry, account, business unit / LOB, product or service category, deal type, stage, owner, risk band, anomaly category, anomaly severity, and cross-sell confidence.
• Clicking a KPI or chart segment should filter the rest of the page and open the related worklist.
• Every chart should support a clear path from summary -> list -> detail -> action.
• Users should be able to save a view and return to the same filters later.
• An Action Centre should bring together risk reviews, anomaly reviews, manager requests, overdue actions, and growth opportunities relevant to the user.

Persona 1 - Sales Executive / Account & Opportunity Owner
Primary goal: Progress assigned deals, keep forecast and opportunity information current, remove deal blockers, and grow existing accounts.
Key questions this persona should answer
• Which of my deals need attention today?
• Which high-value deals are at risk, and why?
• What changed on a deal since my last review?
• Which anomalies should I verify or correct?
• What is the best next action for each priority deal?
• Which existing accounts have the strongest cross-sell or upsell opportunities?
Dashboard, Charts and Visual Views
The dashboard should open with the most important decisions first. Every chart must support drill-down to the account, deal, rep, or business area behind the number.
Chart / View
	What the user sees
	Main interaction / drill-down
	Decision supported
	
My Pipeline KPI Strip
	Open pipeline value, open GP, closed + commit, quarterly budget, gap to budget, qualified pipeline, high-risk value, overdue actions.
	Click a KPI to open the underlying deals. Compare current period with previous snapshot where available.
	Where should I spend time today?
	
My Deal Priority Board
	One ranked worklist combining high value, low closure likelihood, stalled deals, severe anomalies, close-date urgency, and manager requests.
	Sort by value, risk, close date, severity, or due date. Open the deal without leaving the board.
	Which deal should I review first?
	
Deal Risk vs Value Matrix
	Scatter plot with closure likelihood on one axis and deal value/GP on the other. Large, low-likelihood deals stand out.
	Select a point to see deal name, stage, owner, close date, risk band, top positive drivers, and top negative drivers.
	Is this deal important enough to intervene now?
	
Stage Funnel
	Deal count and value by sales stage. Optional view by qualified pipeline, commit, or forecast category.
	Click a stage to list deals. Filter by quarter, account, service category, or risk band.
	Where are my deals getting stuck?
	
Deal Closure Driver View
	Top factors helping and hurting one deal. Drivers shown as positive or negative and compared with the peer/benchmark value.
	Click a driver to see the actual field value, benchmark, and recent history.
	What must change to improve confidence?
	
Deal Movement Timeline
	Chronological view of stage, forecast category, confidence, close date, value, and other tracked field changes.
	Hover/click each event to show old value, new value, change date, and changed by.
	What changed and does my current forecast still make sense?
	
Anomaly Review Panel
	Anomaly category/type, evidence, severity, affected value, recommendation, and current review status.
	Open the evidence. Compare the deal with the peer benchmark or rule. Mark valid, corrected, expected, dismissed, or escalated.
	Is this a real issue or an explainable exception?
	
Pipeline Aging View
	Age of open opportunities and time in current stage, split into simple aging bands.
	Filter to stalled opportunities and open a deal directly.
	Which deals need a status refresh or closure?
	
Account Whitespace Matrix
	Current products/services by account versus recommended missing offerings. Shows confidence and why the recommendation appeared.
	Open recommendation evidence: market-basket pattern, industry bundle, and/or similar-account method.
	What should I discuss with this account next?
	
Cross-Sell Opportunity List
	Account, current products, recommended offering, confidence, methods used, average peer revenue/GP context, and action status.
	Save, dismiss, create follow-up, or create a new opportunity draft.
	Which growth idea is strong enough to pursue?
	

Actions and Workflow
Actions should be available from the chart, deal/account detail page, and Action Centre. The user should not need to leave the screen to create the next step.
Trigger
	Action available to user
	System response
	Result / record created
	
Risk band worsens or high-risk deal appears
	Open deal review; confirm stage, close date, forecast category, confidence, value, and next step.
	System saves updated fields and recalculates/refreshes the visible risk when the next data refresh occurs.
	Deal review with timestamp and updated next step.
	
Deal is stalled / aging
	Create follow-up with action, owner, due date, and customer next step.
	Action appears in personal Action Centre and can generate an alert before due date.
	Tracked follow-up action.
	
Anomaly appears
	Review evidence; select Valid / Corrected / Expected / Dismissed / Escalate; add reason.
	System keeps decision, note, reviewer, and timestamp with the anomaly.
	Anomaly review record and status.
	
Negative deal driver is identified
	Create a recovery task such as qualification refresh, pricing review, technical workshop, or customer meeting.
	Task is linked to the deal and shown in priority board until closed.
	Recovery action linked to driver.
	
Manager or leadership asks for review
	Respond with latest status, evidence, blockers, support needed, and revised next step.
	Request status changes to Responded / Needs Support / Closed.
	Structured deal-review response.
	
Cross-sell recommendation is relevant
	Accept idea; choose target offering; create account task or opportunity draft; add target date.
	Recommendation becomes “In Progress” and is linked to the new sales action.
	Growth action / opportunity draft.
	
Cross-sell recommendation is not relevant
	Dismiss with a reason such as no need, existing competitor, contract restriction, timing, or wrong fit.
	Reason is stored so future review can distinguish “not pursued” from “not seen”.
	Dismissal reason and feedback.
	
Support is needed
	Request manager, pricing, solution specialist, delivery, or executive support.
	Support request is routed to selected role and appears on both users’ Action Centres.
	Escalation / support task.
	

Decision System
The decision system turns signals into a simple decision card. It should explain why attention is needed, show the evidence, give clear choices, and track what the user decided.
Decision
	Signals used
	Decision card should show
	User choices / decision
	
Deal attention
	Closure likelihood, deal value/GP, stage, close date, movement, aging, anomaly severity.
	Why the deal is priority; impact at risk; top drivers; recent movement; next milestone.
	Review now / Monitor / Ask manager for help / Change next step.
	
Forecast update
	Current stage, forecast category, confidence, close date, movement history, forecast regression anomaly.
	Current forecast versus recent evidence; fields that changed; reason current confidence may be weak.
	Keep forecast / Change category / Change confidence / Change close date.
	
Deal recovery
	Negative drivers, stalled stage, aging, missing activity, value or margin anomalies.
	Main blocker; evidence; recommended recovery actions; due date before expected close.
	Create recovery action / Request specialist / Request pricing / Escalate.
	
Anomaly disposition
	Anomaly type, evidence, peer benchmark/rule, severity, deal value.
	What looks unusual; actual value; expected range/rule; affected business value.
	Valid / Corrected / Expected / Dismiss / Escalate.
	
Cross-sell pursuit
	Recommendation, confidence, methods, current products, industry/similar-account evidence.
	Suggested offering; why it fits; methods that agree; peer context; account value context.
	Pursue / Save for later / Discuss with manager / Dismiss with reason.
	
Account growth focus
	Current product mix, whitespace, portfolio mix imbalance, won GP/revenue context, active pipeline.
	Current relationship footprint; largest whitespace; existing active deals; overlap risk.
	Add to account plan / Create opportunity / No action now.
	

AI-Assisted Features
These are proposed AI-assisted user features on top of the analytics and model outputs. AI should explain and recommend; the user keeps final control.
AI feature
	What it does for the persona
	User control / guardrail
	
Deal risk explanation
	Explains in simple language why the deal is High/Medium/Low risk using the strongest positive and negative model drivers.
	Must show the underlying fields and drivers. No automatic forecast change.
	
Next-best-action assistant
	Suggests 2-3 practical next steps based on risk, stage, anomaly, movement, and close-date urgency.
	User selects, edits, or ignores the suggestion before an action is created.
	
What changed? summary
	Summarizes recent movement: stage changes, confidence changes, close-date shifts, value changes, and new anomalies.
	Summary must link back to the source change records.
	
Anomaly explanation
	Turns anomaly evidence into plain business language and states what should be checked.
	Must show anomaly type, evidence, benchmark/rule, and severity.
	
Cross-sell reason builder
	Creates a short account-specific rationale using current products, recommended offering, confidence, and method evidence.
	User reviews before using the wording in an account conversation.
	
Account briefing
	Creates a short pre-call brief with active deals, risk, recent changes, open actions, and growth ideas.
	Uses only data the user is allowed to see; user can choose sections.
	
Ask AI about my book
	Supports questions such as “Which deals changed this week?”, “What should I review before Friday?”, or “Where is my strongest whitespace?”.
	Answers must cite the records/cards used and provide a drill-down link.

	Every summary metric must allow summary -> filtered list -> deal/account/rep detail -> action.
	
Action Centre
	One queue for assigned reviews, overdue tasks, high-risk deals, anomaly reviews, support requests, recovery actions, and cross-sell follow-up.
	
Alerts
	Configurable alerts for risk deterioration, new severe anomaly, forecast regression, stalled strategic deal, margin/value exception, manager request, and high-confidence cross-sell.
	
Explainability
	Every important score/flag must show why it appeared, key evidence, comparison/benchmark or rule, and last calculation date.
	
Comments and collaboration
	Users can add notes, @mention relevant users if supported, assign owner, set due date, and keep the action history.
	
Decision history
	For each decision card, store who decided, decision, reason, date/time, linked evidence, owner, and final status/outcome.
	
Saved views
	Users can save filter combinations such as “My High-Risk Q4 Deals” or “West LOB Coverage Gap”.
	
Export
	Allow export of the current filtered list and leadership decision pack. Export should reflect the visible filters and timestamp.
	
Human review
	No anomaly should automatically mark a user/deal as wrong. No cross-sell idea should automatically create a customer commitment.
	
AI control
	AI may summarize, explain, recommend, and draft actions. It should not silently change forecast, stage, pricing, approval, or opportunity data.
	
Feedback loop
	Users can record whether recommendations were useful and why they were accepted/dismissed. This creates a future input for model/rule improvement.

Decision and Action Status Model
Status
	Meaning
	
New
	Signal has been created and no user has reviewed it yet.
	
In Review
	User is checking evidence or waiting for more information.
	
Actioned
	A follow-up, support request, recovery action, or growth action has been created.
	
Monitoring
	No immediate change; user wants the system to keep the item visible until a review date or new movement.
	
Closed
	Decision/action is complete and outcome has been recorded.
	
Dismissed
	User decided no action is required and provided a reason.
